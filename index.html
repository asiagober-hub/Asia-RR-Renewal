Why I Built It

This workflow comes from real property-management experience.

In property management, I personally worked through renewal decisions that required comparing market rates, effective rents and resident payment history.

One of the factors I reviewed was whether a resident had been late more than three times when determining whether the renewal should receive additional consideration.

That experience showed me how much time can be spent gathering information before the actual decision can even begin.

I built this prototype to demonstrate how AI and workflow automation could turn that repetitive process into a structured, reviewable experience.

“I didn't start with an AI problem. I started with a real operational problem I had already experienced.”

The goal is not to replace the property manager. The goal is to give the property manager better information, faster.
